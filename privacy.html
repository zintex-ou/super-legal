Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Super is published by Zintex OÜ, a company registered in Estonia ("we"). This policy explains what the Super iPhone app does with your information.

What we collect

  • Planning preferences you enter — store, weekly budget, eating style, number of meals, ingredients you already have. Used to build your menu and list.
  • Account data, only if you create an account — email or Sign in with Apple identifier.
  • Subscription status from Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Technical data — device model, iOS and app version, language, crash logs and basic feature usage, to keep the app working.

We do not ask for health data or precise location, we do not sell your data and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Why, and on what basis

To provide the service (contract); to keep it secure and improve it (legitimate interest); to meet legal and tax obligations (legal obligation).

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — database and backend.
  • Apple — app distribution and subscription payments.
  • Content-generation (AI), analytics and crash-reporting providers, where the app uses them to build menus or catch errors — they get only what that task needs.

Some providers may process data outside the EEA under Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

While you use the app or keep an account. We delete it when you ask, or after 24 months of inactivity, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, or object to processing, at info@zintex.dev. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain the AEPD, in Estonia the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

Children

Super is not directed at children under 14.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
