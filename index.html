What Super does

You tell Super where you shop, how much you want to spend and how you like to eat. It builds a menu for the whole week and a grocery list with everything you need.

Common questions

How much does Super cost?

Super starts with a 3-day free trial, then an annual subscription at the price shown in the App Store for your country. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed iPhone and my subscription is gone

Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore purchases in the app.

Are the prices on the list exact?

No, they are estimates. Real prices and availability depend on the store and change over time.

How do I delete my data?

Email info@zintex.dev from the address linked to your account, if you have one, and we will delete your data. See the privacy policy.
